# The Commissions of the Xianzhou Alliance

## Overview
The Six Commissions are the governing bodies that manage the day-to-day operations of each Xianzhou flagship. While the Arbiter-Generals set high-level policy and military strategy, the Commissions handle administration, logistics, law enforcement, commerce, and more. Together they form the bureaucratic backbone of Xianzhou society. The Commission Heads are known as the Six Charioteers.

Additionally, the Ten-Lords Commission operates outside the Six Commissions and is not restricted by the Six Charioteers.

## Sky-Faring Commission
> "Stars glitter in the heavenly river, clouds stream through the silvery strand."

**Domain:** Internal airspace, interstellar navigation, aviation, trade, and starskiff maintenance.

The Sky-Faring Commission is in charge of all matters related to flight, navigation, and trade. On the Luofu, it is headquartered in the Palace of Astrum under the oversight of Helm Master Yukong. Starskiff Haven, the official entrance of the Luofu and its largest port, is also governed by this Commission. The Xianzhou Alliance communicates between ships via the Yellowbell system, and resonances are received by each ship's Sky-Faring Commission. On the Luofu, Yellowbell resonances are personally reviewed by Helm-Master Yukong and her secretary Xikui.

### History
- Yueyan was Helm Master of the Yaoqing's Sky-Faring Commission during Year 5700 Star Calendar. He witnessed Muldrasil's total destruction by the Antimatter Legion while the Alliance observed. Shaken, he later left his position and became the first master primus of the Disciples of Sanctus Medicus after witnessing Shuhu grant life to the dead planet Rahu.
- During Qingying's tenure as Helm Master of the Yaoqing, the Sky-Faring Commission sent a delegation (including Baiheng) to request arms and reinforcements from the Zhuming for the Yaoqing's Heroncrest Fleet.
- Following the Third Abundance War (Year 8072), the Fanghu adopted self-preservation policies, briefly stopped issuing travel passes, and cut off most communications and trade with other ships. They later reopened.
- In Year 8098, the Fanghu reported being moored at the Lanadi star system, the Yaoqing reported victories against abominations, the Yuque sent surveillance reports, the Xuling reported "Business as usual here," and the Zhuming reported being moored in the Le Roy Ladurie cluster's Stella Yamazaki star system inside Klimt Republic territory.

## Divination Commission
> "Examine omens, question fate, and deliberate the future."

**Domain:** Intelligence gathering, strategy, future prediction, and information processing/preservation.

Through the crystal technology called "the jade abacus" (handed down by Nous the Erudition), the Divination Commission's diviners chart the ships' routes, strategize with foresight, and scry into the past to predict the future. The Luofu's Divination Commission, helmed by Master Diviner Fu Xuan, reviews every decision made on the Luofu — navigation, governance, military, and civil affairs.

The Xianzhou Yuque was the birthplace of the Divination Commission, where stargazers and diviners gathered to observe the stars and plot the path ahead. The Commission uses powerful devices such as:
- **Yuque's Deca-Light Reflection Barrier Matrix** — Draws information from the past and predicts the future by interpreting hexagrams.
- **Luofu's Matrix of Prescience Ultima** — Used for divination, interrogation, and hunting fugitives.
- **Yuque's Cloudpeer Telescope** — The ultimate astronomical observation device in the Alliance; can detect traces of the Reignbow Arbiter's Lux Arrow and send out information.

One of the Commission's unwritten rules are the "three taboos": No insincere divination, no unjust divination, and no unpracticed divination.

Relics of past Master Diviners are preserved by the Yuque's Divination Commission, including exquisite divination tools such as the Ruyi scepter, celestial instrument, hexagram board, and Jingtian's Cloud-Soaring Boots. Diviners of the Yuque always cherish their hands, applying ancestral protective hand balm after divinations.

### History
- **Founded** by Grandmaster Xuan Yao on the Xianzhou Yuque.
- Yuque stargazers produced 152 consecutive years of observational records about dead planet Syracuse-III, watching the Antimatter Legion build up an advantage.
- The Yuque's Divination Commission observed the destruction of Muldrasil around Year 5320 Star Calendar.
- During a conflict on Thalassa, Master Diviner Jingtian determined the outcome and relayed it to the Arbiter-Generals, who deployed the Yaoqing's Heroncrest Fleet.
- In Year 8072 (Third Abundance War), Diviner Fu Xuan proposed contacting the Reignbow Arbiter using the Cloudpeer Telescope. Master Diviner Jingtian was the only person with permission to use it. The Reignbow Arbiter descended and annihilated the borisin fleet, but nearly one-fifth of the Fanghu's delves were destroyed. Jingtian perished in the collateral destruction.
- General Yao Guang insisted on exploring ancient routes against the Yuque Divination Commission's recommendation, citing improved jade abacus precision and potential record-high revenue.
- In Year 8100, during the Stellaron Crisis on the Luofu, the Matrix of Prescience was used by Fu Xuan to interrogate Kafka. The Stellaron Burst caused the Meridian Pin at the Divination Commission to malfunction.

## Realm-Keeping Commission
> "Serve with virtue and good deeds, throughout millennia unwavering."

**Domain:** Administration, law enforcement, population statistics, resource allocation, calendar timing, fraud, riots, criminal investigations, emergencies, and public services.

The Realm-Keeping Commission is Xianzhou's administrative organization and the Commission that residents have the most contact with. It is the general affairs organization with a broad range of responsibilities. Some of its staff are scholars who conduct professional academic research in sociology and ecology.

The Commission's weather pavilion manages environmental conditions on the ships, including artificial wind, clouds, rain, and fog. Officers are managers in the Chancery, responsible for coordinating local internal and external affairs. Diting is used as forensic equipment by the Commission. One of the Luofu branch offices is located at Exalting Sanctum.

The Fanghu's Realm-Keeping Commission is reportedly more competent at registering Vidyadhara hatching rebirths — newborn Vidyadhara are immediately assigned a new residence without standard real estate paperwork (likely due to the Fanghu being an autonomous Vidyadhara dominion).

### History
- During the Year 8100 Stellaron Crisis, the Commission was sent out in full force. They announced Exalting Sanctum was temporarily closing borders and navigation routes, distributed protein rice and emergency medicine, and assisted in investigating Disciples of Sanctus Medicus activity.
- Assisted the Astral Express Crew in investigating outworlder travelers and retracing Luocha's steps via corrupted cycrane recordings.
- Investigated anomalies similar to cloudhymn magic in the Alchemy Commission and Scalegorge Waterscape — turned out to be Warp Trotters imitating cloudhymn magic.

## Artisanship Commission
> "Calm in heart, skilled in hand, observation keen, ingenuity engineered."

**Domain:** Crafting and manufacturing — weapons, ship parts, ingenious robots, artificial intelligence, and all manner of manufactured objects.

Members pursue ingenious skills and strive to complete the "Great Smelting Furnace" ritual and earn the title of "Furnace Master." Shards of the Lux Arrow can be reforged by the Furnace Master for those who are worthy.

The Xianzhou Zhuming Artisanship Commission is regarded as the hallowed hall of the craft. By imprisoning the Flint Emperor (progenitor of heliobi), the artisans obtained a literally inexhaustible power source. On the Zhuming, artisanship is the supreme school of learning — all its generals also held the title of Furnace Master. More than half of the weapons used in the Xianzhou's hunts throughout the fleet's history were forged on the Zhuming, making the Flamewheel Forge the holy pilgrimage site for such technologies in the entire cosmos.

The verses *"Smelt for a thousand stars; quicken with steel of heroes. The forge's light boosts power and might; and armaments guard both hearth and meal"* hang in the main hall of the Flamewheel Forge. The Hall of Jadeology keeps close watch over jade carving, making it difficult for outworlders to observe.

On the Luofu, documents, blueprints, and manuscripts are stored in the Sapientia Academe of the Luofu Artisanship Commission, which also handles R&D. The Zhuming's Temperaflame Office takes "newborn" harmless heliobi from the Ten-Lords Commission and matches them with various artifacts.

### History
- An ancient emperor of the Primeval Imperium gifted Grand Artisan Yang Di with a weapons-casting mold for the Zhuming's maiden voyage, still displayed in the Flamedisk Forge Palace.
- After the Heliobi Wars, the defeated Flint Emperor was imprisoned as the Zhuming's power source. The Luofu's Creation Furnace was also rumored to be powered by an imprisoned heliobus.
- After the Crisis of Belief (Years 3400-4000), the Firmamend Temple became affiliated with the Zhuming Artisanship Commission.
- It took the Yaoqing's Artisanship Commission and 3,000 Vidyadhara craftsmen over 20 years to build the Caelorum Venti Pavilion in Moon Delve, hailed as the epitome of Yaoqing Vidyadhara architecture.
- General Yao Guang criticized the Yuque Artisanship Commission for wanting to enlarge jade abaci instead of miniaturizing them, comparing their rigidity unfavorably to IPC efficiency.
- Master Gongshu of the Luofu developed the Luminflux Pyxis, a weapon utilizing heliobus energy.

## Alchemy Commission
> "Temperament and spirit fuse, as the dead springs back to life."

**Domain:** Biological and medical research, healing, medical qualifications, and pharmaceutical regulation.

In ancient times, Xianzhou ancestors were not immortal — they set sail to seek divine medicine. After gaining immortality via the Ambrosial Arbor left by Yaoshi, the Alchemy Commission demonstrated the way of immortality. Due to this, it has long had ties to Abundance. Now in decline, it has become a place where Outworlders seek medical treatment. The Commission holds authority over medical qualifications, administers alchemist exams, and licenses medicines to businesses. Some members serve as military healers accompanying Cloud Knights.

### Medical Practices
Licensed medicines include Health Detox Pill, Jade Marrow Diffusion, Extract of Medicinal Herbs, and various infusions and pellets. An extract called "Tumbledust" from the flower "Yabruh" is used during surgery — a tiny dose numbs the body's senses for painless operations. Cloud Knights use pellets with trace amounts for battlefield pain suppression.

### Medicinal Schools

**Ranzhi School (Yaoqing):** Specializes in food therapy — "medicine is food, and vice versa." Jiaoqiu, a practitioner, invented the "nine-square grid" cauldron-based medicinal formula.

**Piercing Gaze Sect (Zhuming):** Uses acupuncture, aromatherapy, and pyropathy. Can purge evil when the connection between a person and a heliobus becomes unstable. Practitioners include Ardens Regia (Zhuming High Elder) and Lingsha (Luofu Cauldron Master). The Zhuming's "Forget All Worries" incense (sleeping powder + flame ambergris + sunset cinders) calms anxiety.

### History
- After receiving the Ambrosial Arbor, the Alchemy Commission was the Luofu's most vital commission, changing Xianzhou natives into long-life species. They erected elixir crucibles to absorb the Arbor's power at Evemist Mansion.
- In Year 3400, the Reignbow Arbiter destroyed the Ambrosial Arbor. The Commission underwent profound transformation but members never stopped investigating the Arbor's remains.
- The Luofu failed to re-allocate power from the Commission or monitor its members — oversights that laid the foundation for the Year 8100 crisis.
- In Year 6610, the Scripture of Yellow Pneuma Yang Essence was discovered. After debate, Cauldron Master Baize had the original destroyed but content reproduced and sealed, restricted to senior officials only.
- Around Year 8072, Cauldron Master Yunhua was exiled to the Zhuming for restoring Dan Heng's past-life memories. Chief Alchemist Dan Shu, whose friend Yufei died in the Third Abundance War, joined the Disciples of Sanctus Medicus and spread their influence within the Commission.
- In Year 8100, during the Ambrosial Arbor Crisis, many Commission members were found tied to the Disciples of Sanctus Medicus. Dan Shu was replaced by Yuluo. Lingsha was directly appointed by Alliance higher-ups as the new Cauldron Master to purge corruption.

## Cloud Knights
> "Split the sky, shatter the stars, draw back the bow, and launch the hunt."

**Domain:** Military affairs — the armed forces of the Xianzhou Alliance, responsible for wiping out the Denizens of Abundance.

Each Xianzhou ship has a general commanding the army, with a marshal overseeing all generals. The Cloud Knights regard the almost impossible task of ending undead corruption among the stars as their duty.

### Organization

**Command:** Led by the Seven Arbiter-Generals. Every ship operates individually but Cloud Knights are all commanded by the Marshal. Transfers between ships are normal. Appointments are assigned from the Marshal's Office.

**Operating Forces:** Organized into brigades known as fleets. The Luofu has at least four brigades (over 63,000 fighter starskiffs and 120,000+ pilots). The Yaoqing, known for military prowess, mainly uses expeditionary fleets to avoid endangering the main ship. Notable forces include:
- **Verdant Knights (Yaoqing)** — Elite armed forces mainly comprised of Foxians
- **Shadow Guards (Yaoqing)** — Reconnaissance, intelligence gathering, and wetwork
- **Ebonpearl Fleet (Fanghu)** — Vidyadhara cloudhymn magi adept in water and combat magic
- **Echojade Fleet (Yuque)** — Defense fleet
- **Dawn Army (Zhuming)** — Deploy into battle with heliobi; known for artillery
- **Heroncrest Fleet & Red Ballad Fleet (Yaoqing)** — Patrol fleets

Cloud Knights may be deployed on secondment to the Ten-Lords Commission, and personnel must prioritize a Judge's orders when commandeered.

### Martial Arts
- A Cloud Knight saying: "A Cloud Knight must never let slip their weapon, nor let slacken their form."
- **Luofu swordplay** — Swift and agile, emphasis on footwork. Good for those with quick hands and flexible bodies.
- **Zhuming swordplay** — Emphasis on strong, focused strikes, aiming to take down targets with a single blow. Develops strength.
- In martial prowess, the average Cloud Knight matches even the most elite Silvermane squads (according to Luka).

### Equipment

**Weapons:** Devastator glaives, thunder crossbows, recurve bows, rifles, shields, spears, guillotine blades, swords, and personal weapons. Despite esoteric designs, Xianzhou weapons are actually quite advanced — blades are high-frequency and monomolecular, crossbows are electromagnetic, recurve bows are thermal seeking, and arrows are self-guided auto-aim. Swordsmiths infuse basic awareness into swords to make them easier to wield. The Zhuming has weapons of mass destruction like Zhuming Flames and Alchemical Arrows. The Zhuming's Artisanship Commission provides 60-70% of all Cloud Knight armaments.

**Flying Swords:** Some Knights use flying swords connected into their spinal meridian from acupuncture points — they need only think to move them, though they may be difficult to control.

**Starskiffs:** Fighter starskiffs make up the bulk of the air force. At least 500,000 fighters deployed during the Third Abundance War. Large fighters need two operators (helm + turrets). Yaoqing starskiffs are bred with biotechnology using pilot genetic data as keys — performance perfectly matches pilot habits. Newly born starskiffs are "furry little creatures" that join the battlefield after growing a few months. The Navis Astriger is a troop carrier that can land and deploy Cloud Knights planetside.

**Tools & Provisions:** Breathing masks or poison-proof pellets (Clear-Head Flowing-Meridian Infusion) resist borisin Lupotoxin. Yaoqing developed pellets against Chiseltooth Pack microbe weaponry and smell disruptors/counter-pheromones against the Sableclaw Pack. Counter-entrapment inhibitors called "Perfume" are recommended before borisin deployment.

## Ten-Lords Commission (Outside the Six Commissions)

The Ten-Lords Commission, also known as the **Long Life Supervisory Authority**, is responsible for matters related to mara and long-life species. It is **not** included among the Six Commissions and is **not restricted by the Six Charioteers**. No one outside the Commission knows where its base is, who controls it, or how many members it has. (Huohuo claims their temporary office at the Pavilion of Cessation is more spacious than their actual base in the Netherworld.)

Those who commit great crimes (such as undermining the Alliance) are held on the Xianzhou Xuling to stand trial before the Ten-Lords Commission and the Seven Arbiter-Generals.

### Four Divisions
1. **Detention Division**
2. **Incarceration Division**
3. **Interrogation Division**
4. **Punishment Division**

### Roles

**Spiritfarers:** Handle those about to become mara-struck. Members live in seclusion to keep the Commission independent and clean from society, preventing risks that could destabilize the Alliance.

**Judges:** Oversee sending souls of the mara-struck and near-dead to the Hall of Karma (believed to be at the bottom of the Xianzhou where souls are kept and studied to deduce when mara will strike). A judge's body is connected to the Hall of Karma — they can collect souls, decide karmic consequences, and bring souls back to the living temporarily. Judges have all links to the outside world severed upon entering; past names and lives become meaningless. When a judge appears, cycranes malfunction to prevent capturing their image. They possess instruments of "punishment and sorcery" far superior to Cloud Knight weapons. Formally addressed as "Your Honor."

Division specialties:
- **Interrogation Division** — Deducing and interpreting the Karmic Tome and Karmic Mirror (e.g., Hanya)
- **Punishment Division** — Designing punishments (e.g., Forest of Swords)
- **Incarceration Division** — Hunting, subduing criminals/abominations, supervising prison transfers (e.g., Xueyi)
- **Flamehunt Judges (Zhuming)** — Capture newborn heliobi using gentle dreams, then pass them to the Artisanship Commission's Temperaflame Office

**Jailers:** Ensure prisoners remain in the Shackling Prison. Must perpetually guard against serious felons and pick up on intentions through words and behavior.

**Secretary:** The "penholder" — handles strict administrative procedures behind arrests, retroactive filings, verdicts, official announcements, and internal marketing.

**Scribes:** Maintain hundreds of millions of files. Staffing occasionally exceeds spiritfarers. Record prisoner life experiences, criminal records, testimonies, judgment documents, and sentence performance.

**Wraith Wardens:** Somber guards and warriors accompanying Judges. They don black and white masks symbolizing netherworld envoys and wield guillotine blades. Similar role to Cloud Knights but under Ten-Lords command.

**Aurumaton Spectral Envoys:** Law-enforcing aurumatons engraved with Ten-Lords Martial Scriptures. Use strength to subdue evils that stray from the natural order. Their authority sometimes exceeds that of a judge. Assist in investigation and trial but cannot make judgments themselves.

### Exorcism Tools
Various talismans, the Lunaumbra Gourd, Harmonic Chime, Mara-Sunder Awl, Oracle Brush, and Evil Binding Matrix Suppression Towers. 70% of matrix seals come from the Divination Commission, 20% from the Ten-Lords Commission itself.

### History
- Year 7379: Imbibitor Lunae Dan Feng was imprisoned in the Shackling Prison after attempted sedition. Vidyadhara Preceptors performed a flawed exuviation charm to placate the Commission, though Jing Yuan implies they were aware of the scheme.
- Year 7380: Jingliu was taken before mara onset and detained. She eventually escaped, slashing through Cloud Knights before being confronted by Jing Yuan and vanishing.
- Year 8100: During the Stellaron Crisis, Judge Xueyi was injured pursuing Blade. After the Ambrosial Arbor's regrowth damaged the Creation Furnace, the Commission occupied Fyxestroll Garden to deal with escaped heliobi. A ghost-hunting squad (Guinaifen, Sushang, Trailblazer, led by Huohuo) defeated the powerful heliobus Cirrus. The Commission then integrated heliobi exorcism training into standard judge curriculum and planned to learn from the Zhuming.
