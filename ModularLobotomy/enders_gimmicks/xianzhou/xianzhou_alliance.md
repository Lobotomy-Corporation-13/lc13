# The Xianzhou Alliance

## Overview
The Xianzhou Alliance (Chinese: 仙舟联盟, "Immortal Ship Alliance") is an ancient, space-faring civilization following the Aeon Lan (The Hunt). An ancient union composed of many intelligent races, they commandeer Xianzhou to eliminate the Abominations of Abundance.

> "Fight together against countless enemies. Be together through endless turmoil. Of the thousand lifespans encountered, I take but one sip and leave the rest to its fate."
> — Worlds History as a Mirror, Xianzhou

## History
In an ancient era of legends, multiple Xianzhou ships were tasked with voyaging across the stars to search for the miracle medicine of immortality. The endless voyage took its toll on Xianzhou, wearing away its conviction and shape over the long years, until their eventual encounter with Yaoshi. However, all that the fruit of immortality brought upon them were sorrows, poverty, and endless war and death.

It wasn't until Lan, the Aeon of The Hunt, returned from the Abyss. Using gravity as bowstring and stars as arrows, Lan shattered the Ambrosial Arbor and put the wickedness of Abundance to an end. After Xianzhou returned to the righteous path, it formed an alliance under the guidance of The Hunt and formed a powerful army known as the Cloud Knights. The alliance's goals were to patrol the world and eradicate all Abominations of Abundance, and put an end to the corruption of the undead.

Over the years, the Xianzhou Alliance landed on a great number of worlds that used to enjoy the grace of gods. They eradicated countless undead abominations. Any civilization that worshiped Abundance had to consider the possible threat of the Xianzhou destroying their planet. This kind of deterrence was precisely what the Xianzhou Alliance is hoping to be — the constant red line for those greedy for immortality, becoming the price that none dares to pay.

## The Hexafleet (Six Flagships)

### The Xianzhou Luofu
The most well known Xianzhou flagship in the Alliance to the cosmos, excelling in diplomacy and commerce. People arrive at the Luofu to seek medical treatment, study, and trade, with throngs of visitors filling the docks. Residents often take pride in their hospitality and etiquette, referring to outworlders as "honorable guest from afar" or "visiting traveler." Though warfare has receded, the Luofu has not relaxed its watch against the Denizens. The Luofu travels between densely populated interstellar regions, trading and conducting cultural exchange between different worlds, signing agreements with the Interastral Peace Corporation (IPC), and helping many worlds ravaged by the Denizens of Abundance rebuild their ecosystem.

**General:** Jing Yuan — The Divine Foresight (Wise and brave)
**Vidyadhara High Elder:** Imbibitor Lunae — Successor of the Azure Dragon, Commander of the Clouds and the Rain
**Plaguemark:** Ambrosial Arbor

### The Xianzhou Yaoqing
Known for its military prowess, the Yaoqing has long stood at the forefront of the most intense combat missions. Foxians make up the majority and are its dominant culture, with foxian gents on the streets telling stories of Cloud Knight martyrs. Pacify Play is a traditional theater performance of the Yaoqing Foxians. The Yaoqing closely cooperates with the IPC for technology and supplies required for combat, leading to cultural influence — many younger generations enjoy IPC immersia and wear IPC clothing lines. Many locals older than 500 say the Yaoqing has changed, raising suspicions on whether it remains the vanguard of The Hunt or has become a warship of the Preservation gone unnoticed. No extra procedures are needed to visit — just a Xianzhou Alliance citizen ID or Travel Pass/Resident Permit.

**General:** Feixiao — The Merlin's Claw (Fierce and swift)
**Vidyadhara High Elder:** Caelorum Venti — Successor of the Winged Dragon, Sovereign of Storms
**Plaguemark:** Lunar Embryo

### The Xianzhou Zhuming
Known for its exquisite craftsmanship and alchemy techniques since it first set sail during the Primeval Imperium era. The fire of innovation and knowledge burning in the hearts of craftsmen on board has never been extinguished. Less conservative with heliobi than the Luofu — if a heliobus is encountered that is capable and has no harmful intentions, it would not be sealed up. The largest population of heliobi can be found here, continuously born from the Flint Emperor. These harmless heliobi are captured before they come into contact with emotions and tamed with gentle dreams before being matched with various objects such as swords, lamps, and vehicles. Residents love to play with fire — it is said the Zhuming will continue to ally with fire, journey with fire, and share its destiny with fire. Its production engineering technology rivals that of Planet Screwllum and Pier Point, attracting countless outworlders seeking to learn.

**General:** Huaiyan — The Flaming Heart (Strong and righteous)
**Vidyadhara High Elder:** Ardens Regia — Successor of the Horned Dragon, Wielder of the Heavenly Flame
**Plaguemark:** Primordial Flame

### The Xianzhou Fanghu
An autonomous dominion of the Vidyadhara, with different customs, culture, and social structures from other Xianzhou ships. Vidyadhara roaming around the Miniature Myriads and Pearlkeepers meditating are common sights. The sound of belligerent dragon chants echoes through space from time to time. Strict travel requirements — a Xianzhou Alliance Travel Pass and a separate Fanghu Travel Pass (applied for 2-3 months in advance) are required. Xianzhou citizens must provide a certificate of conduct from their domicile's Realm-Keeping Commission; outworlders need a certificate from their place of residence and a no-criminal-record certificate from the IPC.

**General:** Xuanquan — The Seiche Queller (Mighty and powerful)
**Vidyadhara High Elder:** Glaciator Marum — Successor of the Scaled Dragon, Tamer of the Ebbing Frost
**Plaguemark:** Miniature Myriads

## Known Races

### Main Races
- **Xianzhou Natives (Homo celestinae)** — The founding race, present on all ships.
- **Foxians** — Fox humanoids. Third major race, becoming the majority on the Yaoqing after the Alliance saved them from enslavement under the borisin.
- **Vidyadhara** — Dragon humanoids. After being rescued from their dying homeworld, they established themselves on at least five of the six ships. Each ship has a High Elder (barring the Xuling). Glaciator Marum signed the covenant on behalf of the Vidyadhara and the five High Elders — they agreed to keep watch over the respective plaguemarks, and the Alliance granted the Vidyadhara the Fanghu to rule as an autonomous ship.

### Other Races
- **Heliobi** — Largest population on the Zhuming, born from the Flint Emperor.
- **Disatta** — Regarded as loyal allies by the Zhuming, the only ones able to survive the Flint Emperor's hypergravity field.
- **Aurumatons** — Populate and staff the Xuling entirely.
- **Ingenia**
- **Outworlders** — General term for visitors from beyond the Alliance.

## The Six Commissions
The day-to-day governing bodies of each flagship (see commissions.md for details):
1. **Sky-Faring Commission** — Internal airspace, interstellar navigation, starskiff maintenance, trade guilds
2. **Divination Commission** — Intelligence, strategy, future prediction via jade abacus technology
3. **Realm-Keeping Commission** — Law enforcement, population statistics, resource allocation
4. **Artisanship Commission** — Crafting and manufacturing weapons, ship parts, and AI
5. **Alchemy Commission** — Biological and medical research
6. **Cloud Knights** — Armed forces, military affairs, eradicating Denizens of Abundance

### Outside the Six Commissions
- **Ten-Lords Commission** — Not included as one of the Six Commissions nor commanded by the Six Charioteers. Responsible for guiding those who succumb to mara, dealing with vicious criminals, and managing life and death. No one outside the Commission knows where its base is, who controls it, or how many members it has.

## The Mara and Immortality
The Xianzhou people were granted long life through their encounter with Yaoshi, Aeon of Abundance. However, this "blessing" comes with a terrible cost — over centuries, immortals gradually succumb to the Mara, a degenerative madness that transforms the afflicted into violent creatures called the Mara-struck. The Alliance devotes enormous resources to delaying and managing this condition.

## Culture

### Entertainment
- **Classical Storytelling** — Present across several ships.
- **Immersia** — A foxian art form utilizing pheromones and magic alongside advanced artifex technology to produce controllable illusions. A form of VR media encompassing all five senses, bearing similarities to video games and films.
- **Celestial Jade** — An ancient tile game originating from the Alliance.
- **Pathfinder** — A game present across several ships.
- **Pacify Play** — Traditional opera of Yaoqing Foxians using different facial makeup to differentiate characters. Features super-realistic stage combat with dozens of actors moving in midair, acting out gunfights and physical struggles.
- **Foxian Drum Chanting** — The youngest Xianzhou folk art. Performers strike drums while singing with rhythm. Almost exclusively a singing art praising heroes, romance, passionate battles, and ardent emotion.
- **Zhuming Flower-Drum Opera** — Traditionally sung by two singers to produce the eeriness of being possessed by heliobi. "Wedding Wine" is the most famous piece.
- **Vidyadhara Elegies** — Traditional Vidyadhara music using only simple castanets, relying on diverse melodies. Generally mournful dirges originating from folk songs. Represent the most tragic aspects of Xianzhou culture — singers tend to perform about loves lost and pyrrhic victories.

### Architecture
- **Luofu** — Monochrome color palette base with red, teal, and grey accents. Row-stepped gables, tiled eaves, grey brick paving, levitating high-rises, carving styles on doors and windows. Pale walls and dark roof tiles accentuate gentle elegance.
- **Yaoqing** — Originally had flying eaves, glazed tiles, and Fallow Earth bricks, now slowly replaced by sleek, monolithic IPC buildings due to ongoing IPC business.
- **Zhuming** — The Radiant Zenith starskiff platform appears chiseled from a single block of moonstone, with honey-yellow color and seamless metal integration — a proud display of craftsmanship.
- **Foxian** — Fyxestroll Garden, a garden delve created centuries ago by Foxian crafters for leisurely strolls and lyrical poetry.
- **Vidyadhara** — Distinct style using delve enchantment, seen in Scalegorge Waterscape and The Shackling Prison.

### Fashion
Each Xianzhou ship has its own distinct fashion. Zhuming residents prefer lighter clothes. On the Luofu, mass-produced and ready-made lines are considered outdated and preferred only by Outworlders — the most popular being the "Classic Xianzhou Floral" series. A true mark of a Xianzhou native is to buy the right one, not the most expensive one.
