# Xianzhou Luofu Merchant Gamemode — Brainstorm

## Core Concept
Players are crew aboard the Xianzhou Luofu, working together as a merchant flagship. The gameplay loop revolves around **gathering resources, crafting products, plotting trade routes, and selling goods** across the galaxy — all while keeping the ship running, the crew alive, and threats at bay. Each Commission functions like an SS13 department with its own workspace, tools, and responsibilities, but the overarching goal is mercantile: **make money, upgrade the ship, complete trade contracts.**

---

## How It Plays Like SS13

The gamemode mirrors standard SS13 department gameplay, but recontextualized around commerce:

| SS13 Gameplay | Xianzhou Equivalent |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Engineers set up the engine and maintain power | Furnace Master/Craftsman manage the Creation Furnace and keep the ship running |
| Scientists research tech and upgrade machines | Master Diviner/Diviner scan the galaxy and unlock trade intel |
| Doctors heal crew and chemists make medicines | Cauldron Master/Alchemist brew medicines for crew AND for export |
| Cargo orders supplies, miners bring materials | Helm-Master manages trade, Pilots fly out to gather resources |
| Security patrols, arrests criminals, responds to threats | Commissioner directs, Cloud Knights patrol and fight |
| Captain oversees everything from the bridge | Arbiter-General oversees from the Seat of Divine Foresight |
| Assistants help wherever needed | Outworlders flex into any Commission |

**The key difference:** In standard SS13, departments serve the station. Here, departments serve **the trade economy**. Engineering doesn't just keep the lights on — they manufacture trade goods. Medical doesn't just heal — they produce exportable medicines. Science doesn't just research — they find profitable routes. Everything feeds into the core loop of **gather → craft → sell → profit → upgrade → repeat.**

## The Gameplay Loop

### Round Structure
A round plays out across multiple **trade voyages**. Each voyage follows this pattern:

```
1. DIVINATION - Scan the galaxy, find opportunities
2. PLANNING   - Crew decides where to go, what to buy/sell
3. TRAVEL     - Luofu moves to destination (travel events happen)
4. ARRIVAL    - Resource gathering, trading, exploration
5. RETURN     - Head back or continue to next destination
6. PROFIT     - Sell goods, count earnings, upgrade ship
```

Multiple voyages happen per round. The round ends when the **revenue target** is hit, a set time elapses, or a major event forces the round to conclude (like a Stellaron crisis or Abundance assault).

### 1. Resource Gathering (Pilots — like Shaft Miners)

Pilots are the Shaft Miners of this gamemode. They leave the ship on starskiffs and go to planets, asteroid fields, derelict ships, and stations to gather raw materials.

**How it works (like Lavaland mining):**
- Pilots take a starskiff from Starskiff Haven and fly to the current system's points of interest
- Each location has different resources: ores, herbs, biological samples, salvage, alien artifacts
- Some locations are safe (like mining basic ores), others are dangerous (hostile wildlife, Abundance abominations, environmental hazards)
- Pilots need proper equipment — EVA gear, tools, weapons for dangerous sites
- Cloud Knights can escort Pilots on dangerous runs (like Security helping miners)
- Pilots bring materials back to Starskiff Haven for processing

**Resources feed into:**
- **Artisanship** — ores, metals, jade, crystals, salvage components
- **Alchemy** — herbs, biological samples, rare plants, alien compounds
- **Sky-Faring** — some raw materials can be sold directly (less profit than crafted goods)

The Kangqu biodomes aboard the ship also produce baseline agricultural goods slowly — this provides a trickle of materials even without Pilot runs.

### 2. Manufacturing (Artisanship — like Engineering + Crafting)

The Artisanship Commission is where raw materials become valuable trade goods. This is the primary production department.

**How it works (like Engineering construction + Cargo lathe):**
- Craftsmen use forges, workbenches, and the Creation Furnace to process raw materials
- **Smelting** — turn raw ore into refined metals, alloys, and components
- **Crafting** — use refined materials to build trade goods via recipe system
- Recipes have tiers:
  - **Basic** — anyone can make these (metal tools, simple parts, basic goods)
  - **Advanced** — require Artisanship role + schematics from Divination research
  - **Masterwork** — Furnace Master only, require rare materials, sell for premium prices
- Quality affects sale price — dedicated Craftsmen produce higher quality than flex workers
- The Creation Furnace must be maintained (like the SM engine) — if it goes down, no power AND no advanced crafting

**Products include:** Weapons, ship parts, machinery, tools, jade carvings, porcelain, aurumatons, luxury items

**Also responsible for:**
- Ship hull repairs and construction (like Engineers patching breaches)
- Maintaining the Creation Furnace (like Engineers running the engine)
- Atmospheric systems and environmental controls
- Building facility upgrades purchased with trade profits

### 3. Alchemy & Medicine (Alchemy — like Medbay + Chemistry + Botany)

The Alchemy Commission handles healing, medicine production, herb growing, and food preparation. Their products serve double duty — keeping the crew alive AND generating trade goods.

**How it works (like Chemistry + Botany + Medbay combined):**
- **Herb Gardens** (like Botany) — Alchemists grow medicinal plants, rare herbs, and alchemical ingredients in the biodome gardens. Different plants have different growth times and requirements. Some rare herbs only grow from seeds found during Pilot runs.
- **Elixir Crucibles** (like Chem Dispensers) — Process herbs and ingredients into medicines, elixirs, and alchemical products. Recipes work like chemistry — combine ingredients in the right order/temperature for the desired product. Purity matters for both healing effectiveness and sale price.
- **Treatment Rooms** (like Medbay) — Heal injured crew, perform surgery, treat mara symptoms. Standard SS13 medical gameplay.
- **Ranzhi Kitchen** (like the Kitchen) — Food therapy dishes that provide buffs AND sell well. Medicinal meals combine cooking and alchemy.

**Products include:** Medicines, elixirs, herbal remedies, surgical supplies, food therapy dishes, anti-mara suppressants, poisons (contraband)

**The trade angle:** Medical products are always in demand across the galaxy. Mining colonies need medicine, military outposts need field supplies, wealthy worlds want luxury elixirs. The Goldstream Guild handles pharmaceutical exports.

### 4. Intelligence & Route Planning (Divination — like Science/R&D)

The Divination Commission is the Science department — they don't produce physical goods, but they produce **information** that makes everything else more profitable.

**How it works (like R&D tech tree + exploration):**
- **Jade Abacus Scanning** (like using R&D consoles) — Diviners use jade abacus workstations to scan nearby star systems. Each scan reveals information about a system: available resources, factions present, trade demand, danger level, and special opportunities.
- **The Matrix of Prescience** (like the R&D server) — The Master Diviner's main tool. Can perform deep scans that reveal hidden information: upcoming market shifts, incoming threats, rare resource spawns, secret locations.
- **Cloudpeer Telescope** (like long-range sensors) — Scans very distant systems but with less detail. Good for planning multiple voyages ahead.
- **Research/Upgrades** — Diviners can research new schematics and blueprints that unlock advanced recipes for Artisanship and Alchemy. Like how Science unlocks tech for other departments.

**The "three taboos" mechanic:** Rushing divinations or performing them carelessly gives unreliable results. Quality divination takes time — this prevents the Divination Commission from instantly revealing everything and creates meaningful gameplay around prioritizing what to scan.

**What they provide to other departments:**
- **Sky-Faring** — system info, recommended routes, danger warnings
- **Artisanship** — new crafting schematics, material analysis
- **Alchemy** — new elixir recipes, ingredient identification
- **Cloud Knights** — threat intelligence, enemy force composition

### 5. Trade & Commerce (Sky-Faring — like Cargo/Supply)

The Sky-Faring Commission is the Cargo department — they manage the actual buying and selling. But instead of ordering crates from CentCom, they're negotiating with alien civilizations and managing a fleet of trade starskiffs.

**How it works (like Cargo Bay + Supply Console):**
- **Supply Console equivalent** — The Helm-Master uses the trade console to view available deals at the current destination, check prices, and execute trades
- **Cargo Bay equivalent** — Starskiff Haven is where goods are loaded onto trade starskiffs for delivery and where incoming purchases arrive
- **Bounties equivalent** — Trade contracts from destinations ("Deliver 50 units of refined metal for X credits", "We need 20 medical kits urgently for 2X credits"). These function like Cargo Bounties.
- **Export system** — Load finished goods onto starskiffs, send them to the buyer, receive payment. Better goods = more credits.
- **Import system** — Use credits to buy materials, equipment, or rare items not available through Pilot gathering

**Price fluctuation:** Each destination has dynamic supply/demand. A mining colony that just had a cave-in desperately needs medical supplies (high price). A world that's been trading with the IPC for months has a flooded market for manufactured goods (low price). The Divination Commission can predict these shifts.

**The seven trade guilds** handle different categories (simplified at low pop — the Helm-Master manages all guilds solo):
- **Whistling Flames** — Refined goods, farming products
- **Celestial** — Art, cultural goods
- **Securocrat** — Weapons, military products (requires General's approval for restricted items)
- **Goldstream** — Medical/pharmaceutical products
- **Plainfeather** — Starskiff parts and modifications
- **Firefly** — Xianzhou specialties from other ships
- **Aquaglider** — Bulk industrial goods, raw materials (largest guild)

### 6. Security & Defense (Commissioner + Cloud Knights — like Security)

The Commissioner and Cloud Knights together form Security. They keep order aboard the ship and defend against external threats.

**How it works (like standard SS13 Security):**
- **Internal policing** — Cloud Knights patrol the Luofu, handle disputes, arrest criminals, investigate smuggling. Commissioner manages the brig and sets policy. Standard arrest → brig → release cycle.
- **External defense** — During travel events (Abundance attacks, pirate raids), Cloud Knights gear up from the armory and fight. This is the main combat gameplay — like fighting boarders or nukies.
- **Escort duty** — Cloud Knights accompany Pilots on dangerous resource runs. Like Security helping miners deal with megafauna.
- **Cargo security** — Guard high-value shipments during trade. Prevent theft and smuggling.

**The Commissioner specifically:**
- Sets security alert levels
- Manages the armory and gear distribution
- Processes prisoners at Exalting Sanctum (the brig)
- Investigates crimes (using Diting forensic equipment at low pop; future Inspector role handles this at high pop)
- Coordinates with the General on military deployments

**Cloud Knights specifically:**
- Patrol routes around the ship
- Respond to emergency calls
- Man battle stations during travel events
- Escort Pilots and guard cargo
- Can assist other departments between incidents (carrying heavy cargo, helping with construction)

### 7. Command (Arbiter-General — like Captain)

The Arbiter-General oversees everything from the Seat of Divine Foresight (the Bridge).

**How it works (like being Captain):**
- Approves major decisions: trade routes, military deployments, large purchases
- Breaks ties when Commission Heads disagree on priorities
- Has override access to all areas
- Can reassign crew between Commissions as needed (like the HoP giving access)
- Manages the ship's overall budget and strategic direction
- During crises, takes direct command

At low pop, the General also doubles as the Helm-Master or fills in wherever needed.

## The Galaxy Map

### Structure
- A map showing star systems, trade routes, and points of interest
- The Luofu moves along plotted routes between destinations
- Revealed progressively as the Divination Commission scans new areas
- Some routes are safe but slow; others are fast but dangerous

### Destination Types
| Type | Resources | Demand | Danger | SS13 Parallel |
|------|-----------|--------|--------|---------------|
| **Inhabited Planets** | Varied | Diverse, competitive pricing | Low | Like trading with CentCom |
| **Mining Colonies** | Rich in raw materials | Finished goods, medicine | Low-Medium | Like Lavaland outposts |
| **Military Outposts** | Military surplus | Weapons, supplies, medicine | Medium | Like Security surplus |
| **Other Xianzhou Ships** | Unique specialties | Varies by ship | Low | Like trading with another station |
| **IPC Stations** | High-tech goods | Bulk raw materials | Low | Like the IPC (large volume, regulated) |
| **Uncharted Systems** | Rare/unknown resources | N/A (exploration) | High | Like unexplored space ruins |
| **Hostile Zones** | Unique rare materials | N/A (combat zone) | Very High | Like syndicate space |

### Known Trade Partners
- **Interastral Peace Corporation (IPC)** — Bulk trade, technology, supplies. High volume, regulated.
- **Belobog** — Cold-climate resources. Trade agreement from the Luminary Wardance.
- **Penacony** — Luxury items, entertainment products, dream-related goods.
- **Other Xianzhou Ships:**
  - Yaoqing — Military goods, foxian crafts
  - Zhuming — Artisan masterworks, porcelain
  - Fanghu — Marine products, Vidyadhara crafts
  - Yuque — Jade abaci, divination tools
- **Independent Worlds** — Varied, less regulated, more risk.

## Economy & Progression

### Currency
- **Jade Credits** — Standard Xianzhou currency for internal and external trade.

### The Cargo Budget (like SS13's Cargo Budget)
- All trade income goes into the **ship's treasury**
- The Helm-Master and General approve expenditures (like QM managing the cargo budget)
- Credits are spent on:
  - **Supply orders** — Buy materials, equipment, or rare items from destinations
  - **Ship upgrades** — Better forges, improved jade abacus arrays, starskiff upgrades, defense upgrades, biodome expansions
  - **Contracts** — Pay for information, hire mercenaries, bribe officials
  - **Repairs** — Fix damage from events

### Bounty / Contract System (like Cargo Bounties)
- Each destination posts **trade contracts** — specific orders that pay bonus credits
- "Deliver 50 units of refined starsilver" — Artisanship fulfills
- "Supply 20 anti-mara suppressant kits" — Alchemy fulfills
- "Chart the Echidna Skywall sector" — Divination fulfills
- "Escort our cargo through hostile space" — Cloud Knights fulfill
- Contracts have deadlines — fulfill them quickly for bonus pay
- A **Commission Requests Board** on the ship posts what each department needs, anyone can help

### Reputation
- Each faction tracks reputation with the Luofu
- Higher reputation = better prices, exclusive goods, special contracts
- Gained by: fulfilling contracts, helping with problems, selling needed goods, consistent trade
- Lost by: failing contracts, selling dangerous goods, hostile actions, ignoring distress calls

### Ship Upgrades (like Machine Upgrades)
Trade profits fund upgrades that benefit the whole crew:

| Upgrade | Effect | Department Helped |
|---------|--------|-------------------|
| **Forge Improvements** | More/better crafting recipes, faster production | Artisanship |
| **Jade Abacus Arrays** | Better scan range and accuracy | Divination |
| **Starskiff Fleet** | More starskiffs, faster travel, more cargo capacity | Sky-Faring |
| **Armory Expansion** | Better Cloud Knight equipment and weapons | Security |
| **Biodome Expansion** | More baseline resource production | Alchemy (herbs) + all |
| **Elixir Lab Upgrade** | Advanced alchemy recipes, better purity | Alchemy |
| **Hull Reinforcement** | More damage resistance during events | All |
| **Trade Guild Offices** | Better prices, more simultaneous deals | Sky-Faring |

## Win Conditions / Round End

### Primary: Revenue Target
The Luofu must earn a target amount of jade credits through trade. This is the main win condition — everyone's efforts feed into reaching this goal.

### Secondary Objectives (bonus)
- **Alliance Mandate** — Complete a specific mission from the Marshal or another flagship (deliver supplies to the Yaoqing, supply the Zhuming's forges, etc.)
- **Exploration Milestone** — Chart a certain number of new systems
- **Contract Completion** — Fulfill a set number of trade contracts
- **Zero Casualties** — Complete the round without any crew deaths

### Round End Triggers
- Revenue target reached — **Victory.** Crew is praised, bonuses awarded.
- Time limit reached without target — **Partial success.** Measured by how close they got.
- Ship destroyed / critical failure — **Defeat.** Crew evacuates on emergency starskiffs.
- Stellaron crisis / major event — **Special ending.** Round shifts to survival/combat mode.
