A '''Pathstrider''' is an agent who has bound their will to one of the seven Paths. Use a Stellaron Fragment to awaken one. You keep your Path for the rest of the round and cannot change it.

Pathstriders fight completely differently from normal agents. Your EGO armour stops working, your weapon does no ordinary damage, and everything runs off your Path's own stats.

== The turn loop ==

This is the part everyone gets wrong at first. Read it once and it makes sense.

Your weapon '''glows gold when your turn is ready''', and there is a chime. On your turn you may do '''one''' of these:

* '''Attack.''' Your first swing hits at full strength and gives you 1 Action Point plus Energy.
* '''Use your Skill''' (attack yourself with the weapon). This spends 1 Action Point.

You cannot do both in the same turn. After you act the glow goes out and the gem on the right of your screen counts down to your next turn.

You can keep swinging between turns, but follow-up swings hit for a fraction of the damage. Those extra swings are not a mistake, they just are not where your damage comes from. Wait for the glow.

A turn is 5 seconds at 100 SPD. Higher SPD means shorter turns.

'''If your Skill finds no target, nothing is spent.''' Your turn and Action Point stay where they were, so a mistimed Skill costs you nothing.

== Ultimate ==

Your Ultimate is a HUD button, not a turn action, so you can fire it whenever it is charged. It costs your whole Energy bar. Attacking and using Skills fills it. You get a chime and a message the moment it becomes usable.

Energy needed varies by Path: Abundance 90, Hunt 100, Erudition 110, Destruction, Nihility and Preservation 120, Harmony 130.

== Allies ==

'''Designate your teammates. This matters more than anything else in this article.'''

Use the Designate Ally button on everyone you are working with. Allies:

* are '''never''' hit by your area abilities, and you are never hit by theirs
* can be targeted by your support Skills

If two Pathstriders designate '''each other''', the designation is mutual and you '''share Action Points''' - when one of you gains a point, so does the other.

Designated allies carry a small gold diamond over their head, visible only to the players involved. A hollow diamond means one-way. A filled diamond with blue ticks means mutual, so AP sharing is live.

If you are playing a support Path, you can pick a '''FOCUS''' entry in the same menu. Single-target support then prioritises that ally instead of whoever happens to be nearest.

== Armour ==

Your Path coat is '''cosmetic'''. It has no armour value and is not broken.

Your protection is your Path's '''DEF''' stat, which converts into flat damage reduction. Examine your weapon or open the Path Screen to see the percentage. Low-level Pathstriders get a small extra cushion that fades out as you level.

== Levelling ==

You gain Path EXP by consuming refined books, not by fighting directly.

Your level is limited by '''two''' caps, whichever is lower:

# '''Your attributes.''' The average of Fortitude, Prudence, Temperance and Justice sets your ceiling. Low attributes means a low cap no matter how many books you burn.
# '''Your ascension phase.''' Each ascension raises the cap by 10, from 20 up to 80.

Surplus EXP is never wasted. It banks and releases the moment a cap rises, so it is safe to keep reading books while capped. If you are stuck, examine your weapon and it will tell you which cap you are sitting on.

To ascend you must be at your current ascension cap and hold the required Path Material.

Refine materials into books at an '''Omni-Synthesizer''': Traveler's Notes (1,000 EXP), then three of those into an Adventurer's Log (5,000), then three of those into a Pathstrider's Guide (20,000).

You do not need to withdraw anything to spend it. Stand next to a Synthesizer and materials banked inside count toward ascensions and traces.

== Calyxes ==

When a meltdown happens without an ordeal, Calyxes bloom around the facility. Touch one to tear it open and it pours out Fragmentum Touched creatures, which drop Trace Material when killed. They hit hard. Ignored Calyxes wither on their own.

Calyx danger tiers unlock as your facility clears ordeal tiers.

== Traces ==

The Path Screen holds your trace tree: three branches plus one ungated node at the bottom. Most nodes are flat stat increases. Each branch begins with a '''bonus ability''' that changes how your kit behaves, and branch heads unlock at ascension 1, 3 and 5.

Levelling your four abilities also happens here and costs materials.
