05 August 2026

EnderMage99 updated:

  • New ghost game mode — Refraction Railway, an authored boss-rush at the refraction hub. Re-runnable, party-based, with its own leaderboard.
  • Starlight currency + Starlight Extraction gacha for cosmetic ID card skins (1-pull 50 SL / 10-pull 500 SL; 10-pulls guarantee a rare; duplicates refund Starlight).
  • Two lines — Nova Flare (3 sectors, intro line) and Curtain Call (5-act play themed line, every node a boss fight, ending on a multi-wave Serio Zeal finale).
  • Per-mob achievement framework; achievements unlock for earning after the first clear of a line.
  • Records terminal — top 50 runs per line, sorted by total time, with per-sector + per-room time splits and per-player loadout icons. Also viewable mid-run from each sector's advance console.
  • Persistence for mob reveals — fighting a mob once permanently flips its card in the hub.
  • Visual ID Card Skin catalogue in character preferences; job-spawn applies the equipped skin cosmetically.
  • New refracted-variant sprites under `ModularLobotomy/_Lobotomyicons/`.
  • Five Red Mist EGO cues plus seven gacha SFX.
  • New `/datum/refraction_run` / `/datum/refraction_line` / `/datum/refraction_node` / `/datum/id_skin` / `/datum/gacha_banner` datum tree plus `SSrefraction_railway`.
