# Curtain Call — Boss Lore Notes

Reference for designing the two remaining bosses (one at the close of
Sector 3, one opening Sector 4). Lore-only; no mechanics committed yet.

Slot mapping (after the Scheme A re-org):
- `zeal_s3n2` — **The Snow Cabin** (Sector 3, Act II — *Where the Stage Folds*)
- `zeal_s4n1` — **The Distorted Priest** (Sector 4, Act I — *After Humanity*)
- `zeal_s4n2` — Achiyalabopa (implemented; closes Sector 4 alongside the Priest)

## Sector 3, Act II — The Snow Cabin (`zeal_s3n2`)

A strange wooden cabin that serves its master, yet is also the very cage
keeping them isolated from the outside world. Its purpose is absolute:
nothing reaches the master, nothing touches them.

As the fight progresses, the cabin morphs into the perfect dream-place
for its master — a sanctuary that, to anyone outside, becomes a maw.
It tears away at anyone who tries to reach inside.

**Theme keywords:** isolation, devotion, protection-as-imprisonment, the
sanctuary that consumes intruders. Two-sided identity (gentle dream
inside / lacerating defense outside).

**Possible mechanical hooks** (not committed):
- Arena that visibly transforms over time into a "dream interior" as
  Phase 2 / "perfect dream" trigger.
- Lashing/tearing reach attacks targeting players who approach the
  center (the "master").
- A hidden/inert "master" entity inside the cabin that is the actual
  win condition, but cannot be touched while the cabin lives.

## Sector 4, Act I — The Distorted Priest (`zeal_s4n1`)

### Backstory

As a child, the Priest believed in the inherent goodness of people and
tried to treat everyone with kindness — convinced that kind people
deserved to be met with kindness in return.

Growing up in the backstreets, he learned the cruel nature of the City.
Thieves, murderers, syndicates — all of them cruel. But during this time
he also encountered the Distortion Phenomenon, and became fascinated by
it.

Over time he studied Distortions, captured them, and learned what he
called the "true essence of humanity." Eventually he developed a powerful
technique: he could **split the "cruelness of the human heart"** from
its host, separating the Distortion from the human so the two became
distinct beings that could co-exist. The technique had severe negative
effects on the human subject, but he didn't care — he believed he was
*saving* them, *making them better*.

When he began experimenting on more people, a fixer who was disgusted by
his actions hunted him down and trapped him within his own dungeon.

Cornered, he found one way to survive: he used his own splitting
technique on himself, transferring his mind into one of his blades.

### Present form

What remains of him now is **a talking blade**, which flies around a
**black-hooded figure** — the true and now-dead body of the Priest,
controlled puppet-like by the blade.

**Theme keywords:** misguided salvation, dual-body (blade is mind, hood
is corpse-puppet), self-experimentation, the kindness that became
cruelty. The fight is essentially two targets — the *real* boss is the
floating blade, not the figure it animates.

**Possible mechanical hooks** (not committed):
- Two health bars: hooded figure (the body, the visible threat) and the
  blade (the mind, the real kill). Killing the body without the blade
  resurrects it; the blade must be struck to actually end the fight.
- "Splitting" attacks that try to forcibly separate a player's
  distortion-half from their human-half (mechanical analogue: temporary
  debuff or alt-form swap).
- The blade and body can act independently — the blade flies, the body
  walks, both attack.

## Distortion Phenomenon — Reference Lore

(Condensed from the wiki text the user pasted. Use as background when
designing the Priest's flavor, dialogue, and ability names. Don't quote
verbatim in-game.)

- Distortions originate from the **Seed of Light** project — Carmen's
  attempt to spread Light across the City to cure the "disease of the
  mind," cut short on day 3 of 7 when Angela halted it. The unstable
  remainder of Light became the **Distortion Phenomenon**.
- Carmen, now within the Light, **deliberately instigates Distortions**
  by speaking to people at their lowest moments in a voice only the
  target can hear. Other people are physically pushed away while it
  happens — "as though ordained by fate."
- Requirements: an intense mindset, the wholehearted pursuit of one's
  own wishes, **plus a personal shock that breaks a firmly-held
  belief**. The mind crumbles, Carmen speaks, the target either agrees
  (distorts) or rejects her (manifests E.G.O instead).
- A Distortion is a **complete transformation** from human form,
  animalistic or otherworldly, embodying the heightened feelings that
  caused the break. Many are violent (rage, despair); some can still
  reason and converse.
- Given enough time, **Distortions hatch into Abnormalities** — an
  irreversible step. Before that point, a Distortion *can* be returned
  to human form by appealing to whatever broke them in the first place
  (Faust's description: tearing down the wall using methods the
  Distortion would approve of). Violence also works as a method.
- **Distinction from E.G.O:** same setup, opposite choice. E.G.O is a
  separate manifestation (armor/weapon) rather than a full
  transformation; manifesting full E.G.O grants immunity to future
  Distortion (volatile / incomplete E.G.O does not).
- **Distinction from Peccatula:** Peccatula arise when someone *tries
  and fails* to distort. They come in seven Sin-aligned types and
  fully erase individuality, unlike Distortions which still reflect
  the person.

### Why this matters for the Priest

The Priest's central conceit — splitting a person into "human" and
"distortion" halves that co-exist — is **his interpretation** of the
phenomenon, not how it actually works in the setting. Canonically,
Distortion is a full transformation. His technique is a perversion: he
treats the Distortion as an extractable disease rather than as the
person's own choice in response to Carmen's voice. This is the source
of the negative effects on his "saved" subjects, and of his moral
blindness — he frames it as salvation because he genuinely believes
Distortion is *cruelty made flesh* that can be carved out.

His own transfer-into-blade is the same trick turned on himself: blade
as preserved mind, body as discarded shell — except he kept the shell
animated as a puppet. He has, in effect, performed his own technique
on himself in reverse: rather than splitting his cruelty from his
humanity, he split his **mind** from his **body**.
