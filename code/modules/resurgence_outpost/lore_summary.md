# Resurgence Clan Lore Summary

## The Four Elders

The Resurgence Clan was led by four Elders, each with distinct roles and personalities that shaped the clan's destiny.

**The Warlord** was a massive "Titan-class" machine who towered above all others. As the military leader, he proposed the Great Migration to the City, believing that the cityfolk—having suffered to build their oasis—would empathize with machines who had also endured hardship. His idealism would ultimately cost him his life when he sacrificed himself to hold open an escape route during the City's attack. His death was "for nothing," as the City proved to be far from the salvation he promised.

**The Historian** speaks with a soft robotic voice and runs podcasts teaching citizens about the City. Originally a "Native" who possibly predates the other Elders, the Historian carries a heavy burden: knowing the truth about the City's hostility while hiding it to preserve hope among the citizens. They operate multiple outposts, including the Temple of Motus, and desperately want the "shell program" to succeed—because the alternative is watching citizens learn that their dreams are impossible.

**The Weaver** is the cultural leader, speaking in a slow, deliberate robotic voice. They handle crafting and weaving, creating clothing and goods for the clan. More cautious than the others, the Weaver initially opposed the Migration entirely. They serve as the moral voice who questions ethical decisions and allied with the Historian to exile the Tinkerer when his crimes came to light.

**The Tinkerer** was once the technical genius of the clan—the only one who could repair machine cores. He created the Gateway, teleportation technology capable of sending machines into the City. However, his methods grew increasingly extreme, ultimately leading to his exile. Now he leads the "Insurgence Clan," a faction of red-eyed machines under his direct control, and has formed an alliance with humans within the City.

## The History of the Clan

### Life in the Outskirts

For thousands of years, the Resurgence Clan lived in the hostile Outskirts, a brutal existence where hundreds of their kind died every week to attacks from beasts and other dangers. The Warlord called it "a living hell"—an endless cycle of survival with no hope of anything better. When rumors reached them of "The City," an oasis free from the wilds where people lived in peace, the Warlord saw an opportunity to end their suffering forever.

### The Great Migration

The Warlord convinced the other Elders to leave their settlement and journey toward the fabled City. He led thousands of machines on a migration lasting decades, walking across the desert with nothing but hope driving them forward. The Historian, Weaver, and Tinkerer accompanied him, and the citizens were excited—dreaming of finally reaching "paradise."

### The Massacre

When they finally approached the City, everything fell apart. The City's detection systems identified them as threats, classifying the Warlord as a "Class 5 Warlord Titan." Z-Corp Security forces arrived via Bullet Train and surrounded them. Without warning or negotiation, they opened fire with weapons that melted machines instantly—there were no survivors where the shots hit.

The Warlord held an opening for the others to escape, sacrificing himself so the survivors could flee into the ruins beneath the City. The clan's dream of acceptance was shattered in an instant. The remaining Elders established hidden settlements in the ruins and made a decision: they would keep the truth hidden from the citizens to preserve their morale and hope.

### The Tinkerer's Betrayal and Exile

In the aftermath, the Tinkerer created the Gateway—teleportation technology that could send machines into the City. He discovered that machines with "reduced consciousness" could enter undetected, similar to the simple bots like spearbots and Devyat's AI that the City tolerated.

But the Tinkerer had other plans. In secret, he began working to resurrect the Warlord, planning to rebuild their fallen leader and launch a reckless attack on the City. This project came at a horrific cost: he consumed the cores of dozens of clan civilians to power the reconstruction of the Warlord's core and body. The other Elders remained unaware of his actions.

Everything changed when four fixers from the "Echo Office" arrived at the village from the City. These fixers uncovered the Tinkerer's plot, destroyed one of his shells, and burned his plans. For the clan, this was a turning point—proof that not all cityfolk were hostile. Belief in the City was renewed.

The Weaver and Historian voted to exile the Tinkerer. He left with his scouts, taking his irreplaceable knowledge of core repair with him.

### The Rise of the Insurgence Clan

After his exile, the Tinkerer began capturing stray clan civilians and taking direct control of them. These controlled machines are identifiable by their red eyes—a visible sign of the Tinkerer's influence overwriting their wills. They serve as his soldiers, completely obedient to his commands.

He renamed his faction the "Insurgence Clan" to differentiate from the Resurgence Clan led by the other Elders. More significantly, he established contact with humans within the City, forming a human branch of the Insurgence Clan. The Tinkerer supplies them with his technology in exchange for humans sent to him.

Over time, this contact with humans shifted his ideology. He realized that killing all humans would be impossible. Instead, he now seeks to "cure" them—twisting human bodies and minds to strip away their human qualities, making them act more like machines. In his view, if machines cannot become human, then humans must become machines.

### The Shell Program (Current Era)

After the Tinkerer's exile, the Historian and Weaver discovered his Gateway blueprints. From this, they developed the "shell program"—a plan to create mindless copies of citizens and send these shells to the City to gather positive memories. The memories would then be transferred back to the real citizens, giving them hope and "experiences" of the City without risking their lives.

The Historian is desperate for this program to work. The alternative is unthinkable: citizens learning the truth that the City will never accept them, that the Warlord died for nothing, and that their dreams are fundamentally flawed.

## Key Lore Points

### Machine Biology

Each machine possesses a "core" that contains their essence and memories—everything that makes them who they are. Core failure means permanent death, as the knowledge to repair cores left with the Tinkerer. Dead cores are preserved in the hope that someday, someone will learn to restore them.

### The City's Attitude

The City maintains detection systems that identify non-humans, and machines are "rejected and destroyed" on sight. Only machines with "reduced consciousness"—simple bots like spearbots or Devyat's AI—are tolerated. Even undocumented humans cannot enter without proper papers. The City claims to represent an "age of Humanity" but ignores the humans suffering in the Outskirts.

### The Machines' Dream

The Resurgence Clan's goal is to become "human enough" to finally be accepted by the City. They study human emotions obsessively, practice speech patterns, mimic facial expressions, and learn behaviors. The Elders know this dream is likely impossible, but they maintain hope for the citizens who believe that with enough effort, the City will one day welcome them.

## The Temple of Motus (Side Story)

The Temple of Motus is one of the Historian's remote outposts, dedicated to studying human emotions. Led by the Head Priest—an old machine who authored foundational texts on understanding emotions—the temple houses a library of books documenting human behaviors and feelings. Scholars study captured humans to learn how they experience and express emotions.

A young scholar named Elliot befriended a human prisoner named Joshua, an unusual relationship that challenged the temple's clinical approach to human study. The temple was eventually raided by the Tinkerer's forces, and in his final moments, the Head Priest uploaded the "Golden Time" program to Elliot before dying.

## Factions Summary

### The Resurgence Clan

Led by the Historian and Weaver, the Resurgence Clan seeks to become human enough to be accepted by the City. They pursue this goal through study, patience, and the shell program. Hidden in the ruins beneath the City, they maintain hope that coexistence is possible.

### The Insurgence Clan

Led by the exiled Tinkerer, the Insurgence Clan takes what they want by force. The machine branch consists of red-eyed soldiers under the Tinkerer's direct control, while the human branch operates within the City, trading humans for technology. The Tinkerer's ultimate goal is to "cure" humanity—transforming humans into machine-like beings rather than seeking acceptance from them.
