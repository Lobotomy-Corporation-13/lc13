== Tips and Tricks ==
{| class="wikitable"
|+
!Role Tips
!What you can do!
|-
|Command
|As command, you're in charge of everything going on. Most people don't know what they're doing, so remind them often of how the modes mechanics work.
Do a briefing, and during it make sure you let people know they need to stay next to the oil wells to actually generate materials!
Keep Production and Acquisitions from fighting each other for tools, they'll need to work together, not against each other.
|-
|Combat Engineers
|You're the Rooks! Much like a Rook in chess, you're here to protect the 'king', or in this case, the materials.
You're in charge of setting up defenses. The tools of the trade are your sandbags and your emplacements which are manned turrets.
You'll start with a limited amount of sandbags, so split it up fairly amongst the team.
During briefing, try to figure out what Robins squad you'll be going with, either the commander will tell you who you're with, or you'll just need to ask.
Sandbags can be shot through if you're RIGHT next to them, but you'll shoot the bags if you're not next to them, build accordingly and don't cut off firing lanes with your sand.bags.
|-
|Acquisitions 
|You're the one in charge of setting up wells,  and so you will need to know how to work with these machines. Tools are limited in supply so make sure you share them with Production.
To place the conveyors you'll be using, hold them in your hand and click on the ground. If you need to adjust the direction it's going, use a wrench.
To pick it up you'll use a crowbar. After they're down you'll need to use a lever on it.
|-
|Production
|As production you'll be working together to make supplies for EVERYONE. Starting out, try to  focus on defenses and more conveyors. People will need first aid kits, sandbags, and better weapons.
After you are getting enough materials, start working on radios, but don't go crazy with it. As things develop just try and ask what people need. If you don't know what to build, ask a member of command.
|-
|Support
|This is a fast and loose role, focus on what the team needs most at that moment. Messengers should stick with squads, preferably one each. 
When radios start getting manufactured, try to distribute radios to each of the squad and role leaders.

The Raven MPs and Raven Captain take orders directly from the Operations Commander. When you do not have direct orders, assist as needed. 

If there aren't enough people, MPs can work as additional messengers, but do so only with permission from your team captain or superior officer. Protecting the base should be your priority

== Biological Research System ==

The R-Corp Biological Research Station allows you to unlock advanced pyrotechnic, toxic, and electromagnetic weaponry by processing body parts from fallen enemies.

=== Research Machine ===

'''Location:''' Found at the R-Corp base

'''How to Use:'''
# Alt-click the research machine to open the interface
# Insert body parts (from enemies) into the machine
# Select a research project from the tree
# Click "Process Part", "Process All", or click on a specific sample to process it
# Once you have enough points, the machine dispenses the unlocked item

'''Sample Effectiveness Display:'''
When you select a research project, the Samples tab shows:
* '''Effectiveness rating''' for each stored sample (Excellent/Good/Normal/Poor/Minimal)
* '''Point value''' each sample will contribute to the selected research
* '''Color-coded traits''' showing which are required (orange), favored (green), or penalized (red)
* '''Sorted by effectiveness''' - best samples appear first
* '''Click any sample''' to process it individually

'''Body Part Quality:'''
* Different body parts have different base values
* Parts from tougher enemies are worth more research points
* Each research project has '''favored traits''' (bonus points), '''negative traits''' (reduced points), and '''required traits''' (must have at least one)
* Body parts without required traits show as "INCOMPATIBLE" and cannot be processed

=== Research Types ===

'''Tier 0 - Starter Kits (Repeatable):'''
* Cost: 1,440 research points each
* '''Contains:''' Resource tank + Combat implant + Armor set
* Can be researched multiple times to equip more specialists
* Required to unlock higher tiers in that branch

'''Portable Factory Modules (One-Time):'''
* Unlocked once, produces consumables forever
* Deploy with 3-second channel, pack up with crowbar
* Examples: Pyro Grenades, Toxic Mines, EMP Grenades

'''Direct Production Items (Repeatable):'''
* Weapons, armor, and equipment
* Research progress resets to 0 after completion
* Can be researched again to produce more copies
* Cost is 4x higher than factory items

== Fuel Management System ==

The fuel system powers all specialist weapons. Proper fuel logistics is essential for sustained combat operations.

=== Central Fuel Storage ===

'''The Heart of Your Logistics'''

The central fuel storage tank is your primary refueling station. It uses a silo-style visual indicator showing current fuel levels.

'''Capacity:''' 10,000 fuel units (starts with 500)

'''Auto-Refilling:'''
# Place factory materials within 2 tiles of the storage
# Alt-click the storage tank to toggle automatic refilling
# Storage consumes up to 5 materials per cycle
# When materials run out, auto-refilling pauses

=== How to Become a Specialist ===

'''Step 1: Research Tier 0 Starter Kit'''
* Research "Hellfire Rooster Starter Kit" (or Venom/Storm equivalent)
* Cost: 1,440 research points
* Repeatable - can be researched multiple times

'''Step 2: Receive Your Kit'''

The research machine dispenses a box containing:
* Resource tank (fuel/acid/capacitor backpack)
* Combat implant (class transformation)
* Armor suit with attached helmet

'''Step 3: Install Implant'''

'''Self-Installation (Recommended):'''
# Hold the implant in your hand
# Use it on yourself (click yourself or press Z)
# Wait 5 seconds for the insertion process
# You will be transformed into that specialist class

'''Medical Installation:'''
# Alternatively, have a medical officer install the combat implant via brain surgery

'''Important Notes:'''
* '''Permanent transformation''' - cannot be removed safely
* Locks you out of normal weapons (specialist weapons only)
* Only one specialist implant can be installed at a time

'''Step 4: Equip Your Gear'''

# Wear the armor (requires implant to equip)
# Wear the resource tank on your back
# Obtain specialist weapons through research

=== Specialist Equipment Requirements ===

'''All specialist equipment requires:'''
* The matching combat implant installed
* Available only to Rooks and Robins (all ranks)

'''Armor:'''
* Cannot equip specialist armor without matching implant
* Armor provides helmet automatically (via hat attachment)
* No stat requirements - implant is the only requirement

'''Weapons:'''
* Auto-connect to resource tank when equipped
* No stat requirements - implant is the only requirement
* Use in-hand to toggle connection/modes

=== Class-Specific Abilities ===

'''Hellfire Rooster:'''
* '''Fire Immunity''' - Take no damage from fire, walk through flames
* Enhanced fire creation - weapons create longer-lasting fire
* Wider fire spread on pyroclastic weapons

'''Venom Rattlesnake:'''
* '''Venom Immunity''' - Immune to own toxic weapons and acid pools
* Can walk through acid without taking damage
* Armor protects against all toxic/acid damage

'''Storm Ram:'''
* '''Push Immunity''' - Cannot be shoved or knocked back
* '''Shock Immunity''' - Immune to electrical damage and stuns
* Increased movement speed from armor (negative slowdown)

== Specialist Combat Tactics ==

=== Hellfire Rooster Tactics ===

'''Role:''' Assault and Area Denial

'''Strengths:'''
* Fire immunity allows aggressive positioning
* Excellent at denying territory with persistent flames
* High Justice makes them effective frontline fighters

'''Weaknesses:'''
* Fuel-intensive weapons require frequent resupply
* Vulnerable to non-fire damage types
* Low Prudence makes them less effective at range

'''Recommended Tactics:'''
# Use Inferno Wall to funnel enemies into kill zones
# Thermite Sprayer for area denial and ambushes
# Heavy Flamethrower for sustained close-range combat
# Inferno Rush for emergency escapes through flames
# Create fire zones that only you can safely traverse

=== Venom Rattlesnake Tactics ===

'''Role:''' Territorial Control and Debuffing

'''Strengths:'''
* Venom immunity allows safe operation in toxic zones
* High Prudence improves ranged effectiveness
* Stacking venom mechanics reward sustained combat

'''Weaknesses:'''
* Lower direct damage than other classes
* Requires time to stack venom for maximum effect
* Low Justice reduces fortitude and defensive stats

'''Recommended Tactics:'''
# Apply venom stacks with Acid Sprayer
# Follow up with Venom Launcher for bonus damage
# Use Toxic Mines and Decay Cloud for area denial
# Create "dead zones" enemies cannot safely enter
# Protect your acid pools - only you can walk through them

=== Storm Ram Tactics ===

'''Role:''' Tank and Initiator

'''Strengths:'''
* Highest Fortitude of all classes (+100)
* Push/shock immunity makes them unstoppable
* Speed boost from armor enhances mobility

'''Weaknesses:'''
* Many abilities have long cooldowns
* Close-range focus makes them vulnerable during approach
* Lower Prudence reduces sustained ranged damage

'''Recommended Tactics:'''
# Use Lightning Ram to initiate combat and break lines
# Thunderclap Gauntlets for hit-and-run attacks
# Static Burst fields for enemy routing
# Storm Surge Barrier for protecting allies
# Railgun Charge for surgical strikes on key targets
# Use speed boost to reposition between engagements

== Tips for Specialists ==

'''Resource Management:'''
* Monitor your fuel/acid/charge levels constantly
* Plan resupply routes before engaging
* Coordinate with Ravens for field refueling
* Don't waste resources on weak enemies

'''Team Composition:'''
* Mix different specialist types for versatility
* Hellfire clears crowds, Venom controls zones, Storm tanks damage
* Have Ravens dedicated to specialist support
* Ensure central fuel storage stays supplied with materials

'''Combat Priorities:'''
# '''Hellfire:''' Elite targets, structures, crowd control
# '''Venom:''' Fortified positions, elite targets (after stacking), area denial
# '''Storm:''' Enemy heavies, breaching, protecting squads

'''Specialist Coordination:'''
* Hellfire creates fire zones → Storm Rams can push enemies into them
* Venom creates acid pools → Hellfire creates fire walls = multi-layer defense
* Storm Rams initiate → Hellfire/Venom clean up

'''Death and Revival:'''
* '''Specialist implants are permanent''' - even after death
* Armor and weapons must be re-acquired through research
* Resource tanks can be looted from bodies
* Consider having backup equipment researched

'''For Ravens Supporting Specialists:'''
* '''Power Cells''' for Storm Rams, '''Acid Canisters''' for Venom Rattlesnakes
* '''Hellfire Roosters cannot be field refueled''' - they must return to base
* Carry multiple canisters when supporting multiple specialists
* Refill at central storage between runs
* Stay close to specialist squads in combat
* You are immune to resource tank slowdown - use this mobility!
